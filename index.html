My First Day at IIT Roorkee

PR
P Pranav Raj
• October 2024

Stepping through the historic gates of the Indian Institute of Technology, Roorkee for the very first time was a moment I will cherish forever. As I carried my bags through the lush green pathways of this centuries-old campus, the blend of grand colonial heritage and modern technical vibrancy was truly overwhelming.

Arrival at the Historic Campus

The iconic James Thomason Building with its pristine white facade stood majestically against the sky. Seeing the clock tower in person—a landmark I had gazed at on my screen during months of JEE preparation—instantly made all the late-night study sessions feel worth every second.

Visiting Mahatma Gandhi Central Library

Later in the afternoon, my batchmates and I explored the famous Mahatma Gandhi Central Library. The multi-story sanctuary of knowledge, equipped with high-speed networks, thousands of research journals, and vast quiet study bays, left us awestruck. Knowing that generations of pioneering engineers sat in these very rooms made me feel immense pride to now be part of this legacy.

Meeting Batchmates & ECE Induction

During the evening hostel orientation, I interacted with students from all across India. Discussing everything from algorithmic problem solving to sound synthesis and electronic hardware created an instant bond. Entering the Electronics & Communication Engineering department, I knew I was stepping into a world where I could turn ideas into real-world innovations.

"The first day at IIT Roorkee wasn't just the start of college—it was the moment curiosity met purpose."
